Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Zero Data Collection

OneToolBox is designed from the ground up to respect user privacy. We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize your personal files, text inputs, images, or outputs. All tool executions happen 100% locally within your client browser.

2. Local Storage

We use browser `localStorage` solely to save your UI preferences (such as light/dark mode selection and recently viewed tools). This data remains exclusively on your local device and is never transmitted to us.

3. Cloudflare Pages Hosting

Our website assets are hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process basic server request logs (IP addresses, user agents) strictly for security and DDoS protection in accordance with their privacy policy.
