What is Image Compressor?

An Image Compressor reduces the byte size of PNG, JPG, and WebP graphics by optimizing quantization matrices and removing unnecessary metadata without visible degradation in visual clarity.

How to Use Image Compressor in 3 Easy Steps

  1. Drag & drop your PNG, JPG, or WebP image into the drop zone.
  2. Adjust the Compression Quality slider (e.g. 75%) to control compression density.
  3. Compare original vs. compressed file size diff and click 'Download Compressed Image'.
