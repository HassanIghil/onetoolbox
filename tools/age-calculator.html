What is Age Calculator?

An Exact Age Calculator computes the precise chronological time elapsed between a person's date of birth and a target comparison date. It calculates exact age in years, months, days, total hours, and remaining days until the next birthday while accounting for leap years.

How to Use Age Calculator in 3 Easy Steps

  1. Select your Date of Birth using the date picker.
  2. Choose the comparison target date (defaults to current date).
  3. Click 'Calculate Exact Age' to view your detailed age breakdown and next birthday countdown.
