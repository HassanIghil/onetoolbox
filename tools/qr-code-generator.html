Create clean, high-resolution QR codes for websites, plain text, and contact info.

What is QR Code Generator?

A QR Code Generator converts text, website URLs, contact info, or Wi-Fi credentials into a 2D optical barcode grid matrix. Scannable by smartphones, QR codes use Reed-Solomon error correction to maintain scannability even when partially obscured.

How to Use QR Code Generator in 3 Easy Steps

  1. Type or paste your URL, text, or content into the input field.
  2. Customize code color and background color to match your brand.
  3. Click 'Generate QR Code' and press 'Download PNG Image' to save your scannable code.
